# Material Library (/materials-shading/basics/material-library)

> Browse and apply ready-made materials from Spline’s library.



The Material Library is a quick way to get you started with a variety of ready-to-use materials. Choose your needed category, add it to your scene, and customize it to your needs.

The library is composed of hundreds of beautiful materials from realistic to stylized.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/b22e4e7f-6fc2-4ada-8972-d9b454ff9223.png" alt="image" />

<Callout>
  Each category contains a limited number of free materials. Access to the full library requires an active **Hobby**, **Pro** or **Max** subscription.
</Callout>

## How to use the Material Library [#how-to-use-the-material-library]

The Material Library can be accessed from the Material Asset browser. To apply a material from the material asset library perform these steps:

1. Select an object in your scene.
2. Access Material Assets browser.

   <Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/3cc6e1eb-8c8a-446d-a2af-b28445ce16ef.png" alt="image" />
3. Scroll down to Library in the assets browser.
4. Click on a material to apply it to your selected object, a copy will be created to your own asset library so you can customize the material.

   <Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/fa929fa7-7485-43f1-be40-e0cafa07d56f.png" alt="image" />

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/76048ce4-1d52-49d6-9a22-2a8207316e03.mp4" />

> You can also search for materials within the library, try typing in keywords like: ‘wood’, ‘metal’, ‘gold’, etc…

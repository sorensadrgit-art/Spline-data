# Directional Light (/lighting/directional-light)



Directional lights are ideal to simulate illumination from the sun or in open scenes.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/63102c57-9c79-4306-b2f5-bd6468c4e789.png" alt="image" />

### How does it work? [#how-does-it-work]

Directional lights are based on the directional angle between the position of the light and the origin of the coordinate system. The line traced between these two points defines the direction.

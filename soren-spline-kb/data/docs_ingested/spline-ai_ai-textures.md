## How to Generate Textures [#how-to-generate-textures]

To start with AI Texture Generation, you’ll need to:

1. Select an object.
2. Change the layer to **Image** type by clicking on the dropdown icon at the **Materials**.
3. Open the **Image Panel** by clicking on the thumbnail.
4. Click on the **Generate with AI** button in the Image panel.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/8da8ea13-8243-431c-8f03-4d950fed902b.png" alt="image" />

## Generate AI Texture Panel [#generate-ai-texture-panel]

On this panel, you’ll be able to generate new textures by writing your text prompts on the input field.

1. Click on the input field &#x2A;*“Type to create anything…”**
2. **Type in some text**. Below the input, you’ll find some suggestions to help you get started and spark some ideas. Feel free to go wild and experiment with the results!
3. **Click on the purple sparkle button** or hit enter to generate and apply the texture.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/12d29b06-8d75-4aa5-baca-67ae562df3e8.png" alt="image" />

Additional features on the panel:

* Download the texture as an image by clicking on the download icon.
* Change the resolution of the texture via the dropdown at the bottom:
  * Small (512 x 512 px)
  * Medium (1024x1024 px)
  * Large (2048 x 2048 px)
* Save it as an image asset by clicking on the “Save as asset” button at the bottom

# Play Mode (/exporting-your-scene/play-mode)



Play Mode is where you can see your scene in full glory. Rotate, Pan, and Zoom your camera or see your interactions and animations come to life.

Play Mode shows what you'll see after publishing or exporting your scene from the **Export** panel.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/3c201a5e-6d73-4b99-86cd-42bb822bc19e.mp4" />

*Entering Play Mode and rotating the camera in a scene with animated objects. Press Option + Drag.*

### Responsive Switcher [#responsive-switcher]

From the Play Mode, click on the resize preview toggle to switch to responsive mode. You can change the width and height of the scene to preview how it would behave in real time embeds when changing the browser/window size.

# Integrating with Notion (/integrations/integrating-with-notion)



<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/d7bb6c06-091e-4567-a8f0-8557216db906.png" alt="image" />

Integrating with Notion is a great way to illustrate your documents with interactive 3D scenes.

1. Open your scene and press the Export button on the toolbar.
2. Select "Public URL" from the *Type* dropdown and copy the generated *Public URL*.
3. Go to your Notion page and type "/", then choose "Embed".
4. Paste the *Public URL* into the input that appears.

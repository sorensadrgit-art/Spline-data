# Video Layer (/materials-shading/color-and-texture/video-layer)



The video layer allows you to upload videos and use them as a video texture on 2D and 3D objects.

<Callout>
  Uploading videos is a paid feature, you’ll need to have an active **Starter, Professional** or **Team** subscription to use it.
</Callout>

## How to create a Video Layer [#how-to-create-a-video-layer]

To add a video layer, you’ll need to:

1. Select an object and add a new layer on the Material Panel.
2. Change the layer to &#x2A;*“Video”** by clicking on the dropdown on the newly created layer.
3. Open the **Video Layer** settings by clicking on the thumbnail.
4. Click on the &#x2A;*“Upload Video”** to upload a new video.

You can also save your Video Layers as a video asset to reuse in other objects.

## How to Play, Pause, or Stop a Video Layer [#how-to-play-pause-or-stop-a-video-layer]

When adding a new Video Layer, a Start Event is automatically created with a Video Action, making your video autoplay on scene load.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/977a893f-8d3a-46f3-8bbf-acc6eeeb9377.png" alt="image" />

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/120d0c96-ab77-48c4-a74d-0d98884a4a51.png" alt="image" maxWidth="300" />

You can change the event to another type, here’s a list of events where the video action is supported:

* [Start Event](https://docs.spline.design/interaction-states-events-and-actions/events/start-event.md)
* [Mouse Up Event](https://docs.spline.design/interaction-states-events-and-actions/events/mouse-up-event.md)
* [Mouse Down Event](https://docs.spline.design/interaction-states-events-and-actions/events/mouse-down-event.md)
* [Key Up Event](https://docs.spline.design/interaction-states-events-and-actions/events/key-up-event.md)
* [Key Down Event](https://docs.spline.design/interaction-states-events-and-actions/events/key-down-event.md)

With the Video Action, you can control if you want to Play, Pause or Stop a video layer.

As well as other properties like video Volume, Delay and Loop.

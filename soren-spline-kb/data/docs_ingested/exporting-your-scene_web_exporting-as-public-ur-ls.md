# Exporting as Public URLs (/exporting-your-scene/web/exporting-as-public-ur-ls)



<Callout>
  The "Public URL" link is usually used to share your scene via a link. While the Embed code is used to embed your scene in other tools like Notion, Webflow, WordPress, Framer, Typedream, etc.
</Callout>

1. Once you're happy with your scene, press the `Export` button on the toolbar.
2. Select "Public URL" from the left and wait for the URL to be generated.
3. A toast notification will appear to let you know that your URL is ready.
4. You can copy the Public URL or the Embed code.
5. There are multiple properties that can be adjusted under Play settings, such as the camera, toggles for enabling/disabling orbit, pan, zoom, soft orbit, page scroll, and actions on hover, as well as orbit and pan touch settings, orbit limits, pan limits, zoom limits, and animated turntable.
6. If there are any additional changes on the scene or export settings, click on the “Update Public URL” button for the URL to reflect the latest changes.

<Callout>
  You are also able to check the performance of your scene while exporting. Learn more on the following page: [How to optimize your scene](https://docs.spline.design/exporting-your-scene/how-to-optimize-your-scene.md)
</Callout>

Under Export, Play Settings. You can adjust multiple settings to control how the scene is going to behave.

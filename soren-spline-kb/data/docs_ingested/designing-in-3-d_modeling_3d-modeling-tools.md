# 3D Modeling Tools (/designing-in-3-d/modeling/3d-modeling-tools)



The modeling tools enable you to edit geometries to create more complex shapes.

To edit a geometry, press `Smooth & Edit` from the Shape panel in the right sidebar. After this, the object will be now converted into a [subdivision surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subdivision_surface).

You can Extrude, Inset, or generate Edge Loops:

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/1bbdb048-c4d3-45d9-bc7f-1d97c8193e53.mp4" />

You can add Loop Cuts and use the Slide Tool to reposition the edges:

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/3a5337b5-aead-440a-b6e6-ec9a0b1bfe3b.mp4" />

Also, you can modify and dissolve vertices (points), faces (polygons), or edges (lines):

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/1a997608-7b6d-4c3f-ac2b-1349ef985bb1.mp4" />

Finally, you can fill holes by selecting the edges:

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/f160af37-7f08-4491-bad3-473cc3a2e987.mp4" />

***

The following parameters can be adjusted under the settings menu:

### Extrude & Inset Mode [#extrude--inset-mode]

Controls if the selected geometry will be extruded or insetted grouped or individually.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/ae02867f-e44f-4058-904f-a3319ce00071.mp4" />

### Extrude Direction [#extrude-direction]

Controls the direction of the edges when extruded.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/29954e90-0064-448e-88b2-8470b42ffdbc.mp4" />

### Selection Side [#selection-side]

You can specify which side should be affected (both or front only).

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/47e4693f-64ae-4c2e-a654-600e268af9e7.mp4" />

### Selection Mode [#selection-mode]

You can change the selection mode and adjust the radius of the paint selection

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/1f80b23c-6441-4d43-8f46-69e23c675133.mp4" />

### Hole Filling [#hole-filling]

You can specify if the geometry of filled holes will be Flat, Curved or Ngon.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/17c20af8-893b-418e-b110-c7914bcb994d.mp4" />

# Matcap Layer (/materials-shading/shading-and-reflection/matcap-layer)



A Matcap layer (Material Capture) works by projecting a texture on the geometry. This is useful to simulate complex material surfaces on your models. By uploading a Matcap or selecting one from the Spline Library, you can quickly add shine, reflections, and color to your objects.

Matcaps function independently of the scene’s lighting and perspective, creating a consistent material appearance on the 3D object. This technique is similar to applying a texture that makes the object look like it’s made from specific materials, such as metal, plastic, or wood.

## How to create a Matcap Layer [#how-to-create-a-matcap-layer]

To add a Matcap layer, you’ll need to:

1. Select the object and click on the `+` to add a new layer on the Material panel.
2. Change the layer to “**Matcap**” by clicking on the dropdown on the newly created layer.
3. Open the **Matcap Layer** settings by clicking on the layer thumbnail.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/6b0dcfbf-a483-48b5-bd39-4abd02f97537.mp4" />

## Settings of Matcap Layer [#settings-of-matcap-layer]

### Change Matcap image [#change-matcap-image]

You can select the available Matcaps within the app or upload custom matcaps by clicking on the Matcap layer thumbnail.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/1f728efc-da69-4a10-9dbd-e21d031bdbdd.mp4" />

### Matcap Rotation [#matcap-rotation]

You can adjust the rotation of your Matcap. This property is animatable, allowing you to adjust the rotation between different states to create an animation.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/e31cd4e1-2dcd-4fb7-8cba-bbc622fd825e.mp4" />

<Callout>
  You can adjust the Matcap blending mode so that it affects other materials or textures applied to the same object 👇

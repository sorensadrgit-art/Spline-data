# Integrating with Tome (/integrations/integrating-with-tome)



<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/f18a5f10-e289-4f7d-a7e9-9e935ef4c30a.png" alt="image" />

Tome is an AI-native platform for creating research and presentation materials, designed to streamline sales and marketing efforts through automated personalization, data integration, and advanced analytics.

[Learn more about Tome →](https://tome.app/)

Integrating your Spline scenes into Tome allows you to create engaging presentations with real-time interactive 3D experiences.

## Add a 3D embed to your Tome presentation [#add-a-3d-embed-to-your-tome-presentation]

1. On the toolbar on the right side, click &#x2A;*More tiles.**
2. Find **Embed** and press it or drag it into your presentation slide.
3. Paste your scene’s Public URL.

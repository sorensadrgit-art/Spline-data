# Rainbow Layer (/materials-shading/color-and-texture/rainbow-layer)



The Rainbow Layer creates iridescence effects that simulate those of rainbows or bubbles. This is helpful to create surfaces that gradually change color depending on the view angle or illumination changes.

Under Material Panel, you can adjust Frequency, Movement, Color on RGB channels, amount and scale of Noise, as well as the offset on X, Y, and Z axes.

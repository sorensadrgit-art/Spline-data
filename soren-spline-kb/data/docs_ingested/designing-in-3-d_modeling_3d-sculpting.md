# 3D Sculpting (/designing-in-3-d/modeling/3d-sculpting)

> Shape organic forms with sculpting brushes, including symmetry and brush settings.



You can sculpt 3D objects with a set of brushes. Easiest way to explore the feature is to create a 3D primitive (e.g.: Sphere, Plane) and click on “Smooth & Edit”. Increase the Subdivisions for a smoother sculpting result. Sculpt is found on the toolbar once an object is in edit mode (access it from right sidebar when an object is selected or simply double-click the object).

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/ca29750a-4bb6-48fe-8050-2ba6de293158.mp4" />

The Radius and Strength of the sculpt brush can be adjusted from the right sidebar. Adjust it based on your sculpting needs.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/18abe58f-9f77-403c-9e64-33a93c172b30.png" alt="image" />

***

Following sculpt brush types can be used to manipulate your object:

## Grab [#grab]

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/5959c0d3-f1a5-4730-9d59-908e3764deb9.png" alt="image" maxWidth="300" />

Elevates the surface from starting point by clicking and dragging. Hold ⌘ or Ctrl to push the surface from starting point.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/66199303-fccd-4866-bded-6603eedcc2ed.mp4" />

## Draw [#draw]

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/3a9aa0df-aa23-4080-b587-4ced0d7a0e92.png" alt="image" maxWidth="300" />

Click and drag along the surface to elevate it along the path you draw. Hold ⌘ or Ctrl to push the surface.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/5c5454b4-c71a-4d44-8606-2e0d158382cf.mp4" />

## Draw Clay [#draw-clay]

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/38a72e60-0fc9-42c4-b75b-5c27766a18fe.png" alt="image" maxWidth="300" />

Similar to draw but it has a flat base based on the set radius for the brush. Click and drag to elevate, hold ⌘ or Ctrl to push the surface.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/5a93630a-bf43-4f37-81df-65490bc0c0a8.mp4" />

## Smooth [#smooth]

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/2e09813c-3bff-4a02-a87b-15ce39374135.png" alt="image" maxWidth="300" />

If your sculpting is rough the Smooth brush makes the surface more even and smooth.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/b13ba50f-34a1-4b0c-8af2-b0db2b4db080.mp4" />

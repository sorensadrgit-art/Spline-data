# Change theme on Spline (/basics/change-theme-on-spline)

> Switch Spline between dark, light and system themes.



Spline supports **Dark**, **Light**, and **System** themes so you can customize the interface to your preference.

<Video src="https://docs.spline.design/media/light-mode.mp4" />

***

You can switch themes from two places:

## 1. From the Dashboard [#1-from-the-dashboard]

You can change the theme directly from the main Dashboard menu.

**How to access it:**

1. Open the **Workspace Switcher** (top-left corner of the Dashboard).
2. Scroll to the bottom of the menu.
3. Click **Theme**.
4. Select **Dark**, **Light**, or **System Theme**.

<Image src="https://docs.spline.design/media/dashboard-theme.png" alt="Theme switcher inside the Dashboard menu" />

***

## 2. From the Editor [#2-from-the-editor]

You can also switch themes while working inside any 3D or Hana file.

**How to access it:**

1. Open the **Main Menu** (top-left corner of the Editor).
2. Scroll to the bottom of the dropdown list.
3. Click **Theme**.
4. Choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **System Theme**.

<Image src="https://docs.spline.design/media/editor-theme.png" alt="Theme switcher inside the Editor" />

***

## System Theme [#system-theme]

When using **System Theme**, Spline automatically follows your operating system’s appearance settings — switching between light and dark depending on your OS preferences.

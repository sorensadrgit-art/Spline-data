# Exporting as an Image (/exporting-your-scene/files/exporting-as-an-image)



<Callout type="idea">
  Use frame mode, from the toolbar, to help you visualize your composition.
</Callout>

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/c848eddc-3110-401a-a911-25e4dcd73737.png" alt="image" />

Exporting your scene as an Image is as easy as it sounds.

1. Before exporting make sure to compose your scene with the help of frame mode and the frame overlay.
2. Press the Export button on the toolbar.
3. Scroll to select "Image" export under *Files* category from the export sidebar.
4. Choose between JPG or PNG, choose the Pixel Density Ratio and click Export.

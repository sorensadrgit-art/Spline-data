# 3D Library (/designing-in-3-d/scenes/3d-library)



<Callout>
  All 3D models in the library are free and can be used for commercial purposes.
</Callout>

Our Library currently contains two types of free 3D models: Scenes and Objects.

### 3D Scenes [#3d-scenes]

With Scenes, you can get inspired and learn from scenes fully built on Spline.

Learn how to achieve certain material styles, animations, lighting, etc.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/f52cbe07-485b-422e-85d1-ac1108c06c64.png" alt="image" />

### 3D Objects [#3d-objects]

Objects are meant to help you build faster by adding them to your scenes.

You can then customize them to your liking by changing colors and materials or even the geometry.

<Image src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/509cfbac-e094-4a6f-bcae-6f980a8172db.png" alt="image" />

### How to access the library of free 3D models [#how-to-access-the-library-of-free-3d-models]

You can start browsing the whole library from your main view by going to ‘Library’ on the sidebar.

Clicking any of the items will create a new file.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/0976b870-d15a-4c85-9add-46550ffbbfdc.mp4" />

From inside the editor, you can quickly add objects and models to your scene by clicking the ‘Library’ button on the bottom of your left sidebar.

<Video src="https://cdn.spline.design/_assets/docs/adccabe6-5758-406d-ba46-b729ffbd3544.mp4" />
